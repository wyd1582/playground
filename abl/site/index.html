ABL · Agentic Breeding-value Loop

任何方法都要过的裁判

A judge every method has to pass

Agents 提出改进育种值排名的假设，确定性的门槛决定谁存活。冻结的 GBLUP 是冠军，任何大模型或深度学习方法都是挑战者，只有在同一批候选、同一截止日上配对胜出才能晋级。每一个提案、拒绝和成本都进入永久台账。

Agents propose ways to improve breeding-value rankings; deterministic gates decide what survives. Frozen GBLUP is the champion; any LLM or deep-learning method is a challenger and is promoted only by a paired win on the same candidates and cutoffs. Every proposal, rejection and cost goes into a permanent ledger.
